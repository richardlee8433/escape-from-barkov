How to Play

W A S D Run · hold Shift to walk · Mouse wheel Zoom
Q Sniff — treasure is buried out of sight; follow the scent (cold → faint → warm → hot)
E Dig where your nose says "Right here!" · pick up things you dropped
Digging: press E or Space when the needle is in the green (gold = perfect)
B Backpack · Tab Treasure Book — rarer treasures are buried deeper
Ducks are mischievous: a peck knocks you back and makes you drop something
F Go home at the HOME gate · M Toggle music
At home: E repair what your nose points at with things you dug up, or decorate shelves and tables with your treasures
